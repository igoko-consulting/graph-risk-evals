# 08-go-to-market: readers, positioning, messaging

[in review]

**An applied exercise.** This lab is free and public and has no launch plan. The folder asks who should read the findings and how, in the structure of the Go-to-market course (`igoko-consulting/roamly-gtm-strategy`). Added on 2026-11-22 (see `change_log.md`).

- [gtm-strategy.md](gtm-strategy.md): readers in order, the assumption pushed back on, and a one-pager with proposed channels, sequence and metrics.
- [battlecard.md](battlecard.md): this lab against the published comparisons, sourced from PRD v2.
- [positioning.md](positioning.md): the six components, one statement, and what it must never say.
- [messaging.md](messaging.md): pillars for practitioners and for engineers, each with a proof point.
- **Pricing:** not applicable. The lab is free and sells nothing, and the dataset's licence is non-commercial.

**Why the findings, not the author.** The cross-model review of PRD v1 found that a public lab naming its hiring audience reads as marketing, and PRD v2 removed that journey. Every page here names readers for what they would do with the findings.

**What is missed.** No reader has been asked what they want from the findings. The readers and their order are the author's (owner input, 2026-11-22), and the only reader evidence is one cold-reader review (`change_log.md`, 2026-11-20).
